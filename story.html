About Me

I'm Lakzhmy Mari Zaro, an Industrial & Computational Designer with 5 years of experience bringing innovative products from concept to mass production. My work sits at the intersection of traditional industrial design principles and cutting-edge computational design tools, allowing me to create products that are both beautiful and highly functional.

My approach to design is rooted in empathy and research. I believe the best products emerge from deeply understanding user needs, material properties, and manufacturing constraints. Whether designing outdoor equipment, consumer electronics, or gaming installations, I strive to create solutions that enhance people's lives in meaningful ways.
