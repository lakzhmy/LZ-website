Pet Care Startup - Smart Design

Role Lead Designer
Year 2022-2023
Category Consumer Products

Overview

A comprehensive smart pet care product line designed for modern pet owners who want to ensure their pets' wellbeing even when away from home. This startup project involved designing connected devices, accessories, and a mobile app ecosystem that work together to monitor, entertain, and care for pets throughout the day.

Product Ecosystem

Smart Feeder
Activity Monitor
Interactive Toys
Mobile App UI

User Research

Conducted extensive interviews with pet owners, veterinarians, and animal behaviorists to understand the pain points in modern pet care. The research revealed that working pet owners wanted peace of mind and ways to interact with their pets remotely.

Design Approach

Created a cohesive product family with pet-friendly materials, easy-to-clean surfaces, and intuitive controls. The products integrate seamlessly with smart home ecosystems while maintaining a warm, approachable aesthetic that fits naturally in modern homes.
