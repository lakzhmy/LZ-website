Nose Pad System

Role Industrial Designer
Year 2024
Category Product Design

Overview

The Nose Pad System is an innovative equestrian equipment design focused on improving comfort and control for both rider and horse. This project involved extensive research into ergonomics, material science, and traditional craftsmanship to create a modern solution that respects the heritage of horseback riding while introducing contemporary design principles.

Final Product

Challenge

Traditional nose pads often caused discomfort during extended riding sessions and lacked adjustability for different horse breeds and rider preferences. The challenge was to create a universal system that maintained traditional aesthetics while incorporating modern ergonomic principles.

Solution

Developed a modular nose pad system with interchangeable components that allow for customization based on horse anatomy and riding style. The design uses advanced materials that are both durable and comfortable, while maintaining the classic look that equestrians appreciate.
