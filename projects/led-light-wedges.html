LED Light Wedges for EGMs

Client IGT x Fi Company
Year 2021-2022
Category Gaming & Entertainment

Overview

Designed innovative LED light wedge systems for Electronic Gaming Machines (EGMs) in collaboration with IGT and Fi Company. This project focused on creating immersive lighting experiences that enhance player engagement while meeting strict regulatory requirements for the gaming industry. The modular design allows for customization across different casino environments and gaming themes.

Technical Challenge

The primary challenge was designing a lighting system that could withstand 24/7 operation in high-traffic casino environments while maintaining consistent color accuracy and brightness. The solution needed to be modular for easy maintenance and comply with strict gaming industry regulations.

Innovation

Developed a patented wedge design that allows for seamless integration with existing EGM architectures. The LED system features custom optics for optimal light distribution and a thermal management system that ensures longevity and consistent performance in demanding environments.
