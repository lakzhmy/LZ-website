Bex Sunglasses & Lattice Trekking Pole Handles

Role Product Designer
Year 2023-2024
Category Outdoor Gear

Overview

A comprehensive outdoor gear project encompassing three innovative products: Bex Sunglasses with advanced UV protection, Lattice Trekking Pole Handles with ergonomic grip technology, and the Presonal personal navigation device. This multi-product line was designed to enhance the outdoor adventure experience through thoughtful industrial design and material innovation.

Product Line

Design Philosophy

Each product in this line was designed with the adventurer in mind, balancing durability, functionality, and aesthetic appeal. The cohesive design language across all products creates a recognizable brand identity while addressing specific user needs in outdoor environments.

Innovation

The lattice structure used in the trekking pole handles provides superior grip while reducing weight. The sunglasses feature interchangeable lenses for various light conditions, and the Presonal device integrates seamlessly with existing outdoor gear systems.
